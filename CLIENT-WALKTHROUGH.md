# Welcome Woods — client walkthrough

## Pehle kya bolna hai

“Yeh Welcome Woods website ka design presentation hai. Ismein aapki brand identity, overall look, saare main pages aur visitor ka journey ready hai. Abhi project photographs aur stories sample hain. Aapke original materials aate hi yahi layouts aapke actual work se replace honge.”

## 5–7 minute ka simple walkthrough

1. **Home — first impression.** Opening slideshow ko kuch seconds do. Warm cream, brown, natural materials aur elegant typography brand ki premium feeling establish karte hain. Do clear actions hain: projects dekhna aur apna project discuss karna.
2. **Selected Spaces — actual work ka future showcase.** Ek study kholo. Explain karo: “Har real project ko apna page milega—brief, scope, material choices, images aur story.” Gallery image par click karke large view dikhao. Abhi examples ko completed Welcome Woods projects mat bolo.
3. **Our Expertise — client ko relevance samajh aaye.** Residential, commercial, space planning, styling aur execution ke layouts dikhao. Service par click karne ke baad enquiry mein wahi service select hoti hai. Final service descriptions unke approval se update honge.
4. **The Process — practical confidence.** Six stages ko jaldi walk through karo: conversation, space understanding, direction, scope/budget, execution, handover. Explain karo ki yeh proposed framework hai; exact company process ke according copy final hogi.
5. **The Studio — business ke peeche ke log.** Philosophy aur founder/team profile ka reserved section dikhao. Biography, team names aur portraits milte hi yahan aa jayenge.
6. **Start a Project — enquiry.** Name, contact, city, space type aur requirement capture hote hain. Demo details se draft prepare karo. Current version email app kholta hai; automatic sending/CRM/WhatsApp integration final workflow confirmation ke baad hogi. Real client ko demo email bhejne ki zaroorat nahi.
7. **Notes & Recognition — optional proof.** Awards, press aur testimonials ke clean layouts ready hain. Sirf verified, approved material milne par unhe fill karenge.

## Client se approval kin baaton par lena hai

- Overall mood: colours, typography, spacing and premium feeling.
- Correct service list and preferred wording.
- Website ka journey: work → expertise/process → enquiry.
- Brand name/logo usage, and real project content priorities.

Abhi invented project names, locations, project counts, awards, office claims ya guaranteed timelines confirm mat karna. The supplied five cities still need clarification about coverage versus office locations.

## Jab unka material aa jayega

1. **Project-wise sort:** Drive mein har project ke images, brief, scope, city, status/year, area if approved, and image permissions collect karenge.
2. **Best photos choose:** Ek strong hero, wide room views, close details, portrait crops aur relevant site-progress images shortlist karenge. WhatsApp previews ko originals se replace karenge.
3. **Same layouts fill:** `src/data/site.js` ke example studies ko actual records se replace karenge. Har project ko unique page/link milega. Layout dobara build nahi karna padega.
4. **Story write:** Client ka brief, challenge, actual work done, materials and outcome ko concise case study mein turn karenge. Before/during/after tabhi show hoga jab photographs same project aur correct stages ke hon.
5. **Brand & trust final:** Original logo master, founder/team profile, verified testimonials/press and confirmed location/contact details add karenge.
6. **Launch pass:** Borrowed images/sample copy remove, mobile crops and image compression check, final enquiry delivery/privacy/SEO/domain confirm, then public launch content approve karenge.

Abhi simple content files use ho rahi hain. Agar client regular projects khud add karna chahe, future mein CMS add kar sakte hain; current version mein updates hum controlled content edits se karenge.
